Phishing Email Analyzer

Python • Ollama • Email Parsing • MITRE ATT&CK

What it is

A multi-layer phishing detection tool that combines three independent analysis methods: email authentication validation, URL reputation scanning, and LLM-based content analysis. Each layer catches different attack vectors that the others might miss.

Layer 1 — Email authentication

Validates SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records for the sending domain. A legitimate email from paypal.com should pass all three. Failure or missing records is a strong phishing indicator — most phishing emails spoof domains that don't have these controls configured.

Layer 2 — URL analysis

Extracts all URLs from the email body and checks for homograph attacks (e.g. pаypal.com using a Cyrillic 'а' instead of Latin 'a'), suspicious TLDs, URL shorteners, and domains that closely resemble known brands using Levenshtein distance.

Layer 3 — LLM content analysis

Passes the email subject and body to a locally-running Ollama model with a security-focused prompt. The model identifies social engineering tactics (urgency, fear, authority impersonation), suspicious requests, and unusual phrasing patterns.

Results are output as a colour-coded terminal report with a final risk score and MITRE ATT&CK technique mappings (T1566 Phishing, T1598 Spearphishing, etc.).
